Anhedonia is the problem in one sentence: she can't say what she'd enjoy. Her clinician can still say what she loved before: an artist, a show, a food. That is all Rekindle needs to start, and every case in the app is fictional.

README · The Problem · evidence base for behavioral activation: Richards et al., Lancet 2016 (COBRA)

Behavioral activation is a first-line, low-cost treatment for depression, and its core step is activity scheduling. Sessions stall on brainstorming, generic ideas fail, and a real place named in session is rarely checked against the hour, the distance or a recovery plan.

Honest limits

What it does not do.

These four lines are quoted from the README as written. It is clinician support, not a clinical tool. A week takes about twenty seconds because the coaching step dominates. The deny list is measured but could miss a venue with no tags, and the threads are attribution only.
